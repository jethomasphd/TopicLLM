Public data, private credentials

Understand what leaves your browser.

The demonstration uses released Study 1 alcohol-brand posts. Study 2 individual-user data and collection personnel details are not part of this release.

The walkthrough and browser sandbox

Released datasets and recorded results download to your browser. Local sandbox analysis runs there. A file you upload to the sandbox is not submitted to the public live endpoint; research belongs in your approved local or institutional environment.

Live examples and the research guide

Choosing a released Study 1 post or recorded topic sends its approved ID to the Cloudflare Worker. The Worker selects the public text and sends it to OpenAI. Arbitrary uploaded research text and custom prompts are not accepted by that endpoint. Guide questions send your question, recent chat and relevant public source excerpts to OpenAI. Do not include private research records or API credentials in chat.

Room to experiment, with a firm budget

Guide questions and live examples share 200 calls per browser session and 600 calls per shared network per UTC day. A naming experiment with 20 votes uses 20 calls. The site-wide allowance is $20 per UTC day, with a $620 calendar-month ceiling. These are shared allowances, not a promise of availability to every visitor. Rate and concurrent-request controls prevent overload.

Budget reservations are atomic and occur before provider requests. Completed calls settle at reported token usage; uncertain calls keep their conservative reservation. Controls fail closed if accounting is unavailable. The separate operator endpoint remains disabled.

Credentials, storage and retention

Provider keys remain on the server. The application keeps chat in page memory and clears it on refresh. Signed session cookies expire after one day. The quota service stores daily keyed IP digests, counters and short response receipts, which expire after 30 minutes. It does not store raw IP addresses or raw prompts. Live response receipts can include the model's answer.

OpenAI requests use store:false. This is not a claim of zero provider retention; Cloudflare and OpenAI processing are separate from browser memory. The workflow documentation explains how researchers use their own approved provider environment.
