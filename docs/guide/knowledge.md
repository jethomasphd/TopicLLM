# TopicLLM research guide: maintained briefing

## Start with the research question

TopicLLM helps researchers turn recurring language into reviewable measurements. Computers organize a corpus and propose topic names; researchers interpret those groups and define themes; an independently evaluated classifier measures where those themes occur. Agents run the prescribed Python scripts and retain evidence. Humans make the scientific judgments.

The new worked application asks: **Which themes emerge during Donald Trump's 2015–2016 presidential campaign, and what share of his account's subsequent first-term posts expresses each theme?** This is a campaign-to-presidency application, not a comparison of quarterly trends. The standalone manuscript explains the method and links each step to code. The separate research library preserves the original dissertation and two papers unchanged; their datasets are not distributed.

## One archive, two analytic roles

The canonical archive contains 34,416 records from June 16, 2015 through January 8, 2021. Source: CodeBreaker619, Donald Trump Tweets Dataset, Kaggle version 1, updated January 10, 2021. https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/codebreaker619/donald-trump-tweets-dataset

- Campaign discovery: 7,805 posts, June 16, 2015–November 8, 2016, inclusive.
- First-term classification and overall shares: 26,239 posts, January 20, 2017–January 8, 2021, inclusive.
- The 372 intervening transition records remain in the archive but are outside both analytic views.

The archive ends before the constitutional term ended. Do not claim full capture through January 20, 2021, personal authorship of every account post, or unrestricted reuse of third-party text. Retweets and repeated text under distinct IDs remain posting events. The archive's designation is Data files © Original Authors. It does not establish completeness or verify claims made in the posts. Dates use the supplied timestamp without timezone conversion because no offset is provided.

Discovery preprocessing decodes HTML, removes URLs and @handles, keeps hashtag words, then removes nonletters, lowercases, removes English/domain stopwords and lemmatizes. Empty modeled text is excluded from discovery only; raw records remain intact. Interpretation and presidential classification use original text. Sources and hashes are in /data/README.md and /data/trump/manifest.json.

## The workflow in ordinary language

1. **Discover campaign topics.** MiniLM embeddings, UMAP, HDBSCAN and BERTopic group similar language. Fifty parameter draws and ceilings 2–25 produce 1,200 candidate evaluations. A topic ceiling is not a promised topic count. Coherence evaluates word association, not scientific usefulness. Researchers inspect coverage, original posts and outliers before adopting a solution; any override of the coherence winner is recorded.
2. **Propose names.** The application specifies 500 fixed OpenAI naming calls per adopted topic, temperature 0.5 and a 32-token maximum. Preserve raw ballots and tally rules. The modal wording is a proposal. Repeated responses from one model are not independent experts. Small live examples on the site illustrate the operation without completing this research stage.
3. **Build a human codebook.** Two-pass human theme review interprets the campaign topics, original posts and relevant literature. They agree theme definitions, inclusion/exclusion rules, examples and a primary-theme decision rule. An agent can prepare suggestions and a worksheet but cannot approve its own work or act as the second human.
4. **Develop, validate, accept.** Draw a held-out presidential sample before drawing development data, excluding held-out IDs and exact texts from development. Each set receives two independent human coding passes, completed by separate human coders without access to each other’s labels or model predictions. Disagreements are then adjudicated. The frozen samples contain 100 development and 250 evaluation posts per coding pass. Development and held-out annotation, adjudication and classifier evaluation are complete. The held-out acceptance gate failed. Development selects between Boolean and LLM classification. The untouched evaluation set assesses only that frozen choice. The default gate requires agreement strictly above 0.85, positive support and nonzero recall for every substantive theme. Researchers inspect errors and explicitly accept performance before scaling. Failed evaluation requires a declared revision and fresh holdout, not tuning against the opened labels.
5. **Estimate overall presidential prevalence.** Apply the accepted classifier to all 26,239 target posts. Divide each theme count by that complete denominator; retain zero-count themes and the residual. The primary application requests overall shares only, with no period comparisons or significance tests. These are classifier-assigned shares of archived account activity, not public opinion, persuasion or causal effects.

## Current evidence and honest status

The new campaign search completed all 1,200 candidate evaluations. Of 7,805 campaign posts, 7,779 retained modeled tokens and 26 were recorded as preprocessing exclusions. The numerical coherence winner has c_v 0.6090 and two topics: one broad cluster of 7,390 posts, a book-promotion cluster of 35, and 354 outliers. The largest cluster contains 99.5% of assigned posts. This is a diagnostic result, not a useful finished taxonomy.

The author selected candidate B: 12 topics (c_v 0.5406), 3,925 assigned posts and 3,854 outliers (49.5% of modeled posts). The selected model was reconstructed and reloaded with exactly matching assignments. Naming is complete: 6,000 retained responses, 500 per topic, with a uniform 32-token ceiling. Two-pass human theme review was completed on November 18, 2026 and retained 12 distinct themes rather than merging this particular solution. The source-bound map, full definitions, decision rule and both reviewers’ notes are published in /data/trump/campaign/. The local synthesis tool supports many topics sharing one theme; a one-to-one map here is an actual research choice, not a software constraint. Preliminary attempts affected by output truncation and a prompt-encoding error are retained separately and excluded from final tallies. The approved human codebook is complete. On November 19, 2026, two independent human coding passes covered 100 development posts, followed by adjudication of all 15 differences. Initial human agreement was 85% (κ = 0.809). The first development comparison selected OpenAI GPT-4o at 64% agreement (κ = 0.5438), versus 30% (κ = 0.1417) for Boolean. This is selection evidence, not independent validation; do not call the classifier accurate or validated. The model assigned 41 residual labels versus 21 in human consensus, and missed all seven consumer-relations reference labels. The principal investigator chose to retain the existing codebook and selected classifier for the methodological demonstration and proceed to independent held-out evaluation. Held-out evaluation is complete: the unchanged LLM matched 175 of 250 adjudicated labels (70%; 95% Wilson interval 64.1%–75.3%; κ = 0.6353). Independent human agreement was 68% (κ = 0.6144), followed by adjudication of all 80 differences. The classifier did not meet the declared >85% agreement and per-theme support/recall criteria. The explicitly authorized demonstration classified all 26,239 presidential posts with the unchanged model. Its largest substantive assigned category was Criticism of Political Opponents (6,924; 26.39%); the residual received 10,430 posts (39.75%). These are unvalidated model-assigned shares, not validated estimates of theme prevalence. The model assigned 86 residual labels versus 41 in consensus, accounting for 45 of its 75 disagreements. It missed all three consumer-relations reference labels and the single golf-resort label. Merchandise had zero reference examples, so its recall is not estimable. This evaluation diagnoses limits of temporal transfer; do not call the model validated or claim that a theme is absent merely because it was not detected. Report human agreement separately from classifier agreement. Held-out adjudication notes were not used to revise the codebook or prompt.

An earlier whole-archive diagnostic run evaluated 1,200 candidates and returned three highly imbalanced topics, c_v 0.5898, with 32,725 modeled posts and 1,691 preprocessing exclusions. Its largest group contained 21,073 posts and the others 10 and 6. That evidence is archived separately; the current walkthrough displays the selected 12-topic campaign solution. Live classification now uses the actual 12-theme human codebook. Visitor maps remain separate learning drafts; live responses do not constitute classifier validation. Lead general visitor answers with the complete methodological walkthrough on the 2015–2016 campaign and first presidential term, through the archive’s January 8, 2021 endpoint. The first term ended January 20, 2021; January 8 is the archive endpoint, not the end of the term. Use “model-assigned shares for the teaching demonstration” for its final output. Avoid repeating threshold warnings at every step; direct readers to /paper/#limitations for a focused explanation. When asked about accuracy, validation, findings or research readiness, report the 70% held-out agreement and unmet criteria plainly. Researchers must iterate on development evidence, freeze revisions and obtain fresh independent evaluation and human acceptance before substantive research use. ## Original research library

Jacob E. Thomas developed TopicLLM during dissertation research at The University of Texas at Austin, informed by Gillies, Murthy, Brenton and Olaniyan's (2022) Theme and Topic framework. The dissertation and original studies are historical research in action. Use original page excerpts for exact historical claims; do not transfer their results to the Trump application.

Study 1 reported 5,872 posts from 11 alcohol companies, seven topics and five themes: Alcohol Delivery and Isolation Drinking, Restaurant Support, Social Media Promotions, Sports and Gaming. Study 2 examined general-user posts and information dynamics. The maintained topic-to-prevalence package does not implement every original exposure/GSEM or LIWC analysis. Historical Twitter/tweet terminology refers to the platform now called X.

Dissertation committee review differs from journal peer review. The studies are presented as preprints. No independent replication by other researchers is established. Probabilistic methods, seeds, software/hardware and model revisions can alter outputs; preserved inputs and evidence make those differences assessable. Encourage feedback or replication through repository issues and jethomasphd@gmail.com.
