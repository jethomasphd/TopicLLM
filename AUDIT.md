# Research sources and release evidence

The dissertation (Thomas, 2025) is authoritative for historical research claims. Its two studies show the methodology in action. Original dissertation, paper editions and supplements are protected byte for byte by source-integrity.json. Run `node scripts/verify-sources.mjs` to check them.

The maintained methods paper is a separate, identified edition. It explains the reusable methodology through the public Donald Trump Twitter archive, from the 2015 campaign into the first presidency. The one tutorial corpus has 34,416 posts, June 16, 2015 through January 8, 2021. Its provenance, exact selection and hashes are in docs/data/trump/manifest.json. Dissertation datasets are not part of the release.

Campaign discovery, naming and two-pass human theme review are complete. The approved 12-theme codebook, map and original reviewer submission are preserved under docs/data/trump/campaign/. Live calls use that codebook; individual responses and visitor maps do not constitute classifier validation. Development annotation and adjudication are complete for 100 posts. Independent human agreement was 85% (κ = 0.809); all 15 differences were adjudicated. The first development comparison selected LLM (64% agreement) over Boolean (30%). Held-out evaluation is complete: the unchanged LLM matched 175 of 250 adjudicated labels (70%; 95% Wilson interval 64.1%–75.3%; κ = 0.6353). Independent human agreement was 68% (κ = 0.6144), followed by adjudication of all 80 differences. The classifier did not meet the declared >85% agreement and per-theme support/recall criteria. The explicitly authorized demonstration classified all 26,239 presidential posts with the unchanged model. Its largest substantive assigned category was Criticism of Political Opponents (6,924; 26.39%); the residual received 10,430 posts (39.75%). These are unvalidated model-assigned shares, not validated estimates of theme prevalence. The research gates require those inputs before corpus classification and inference. Never compare tutorial output to the dissertation as though the two corpora should yield the same findings.
