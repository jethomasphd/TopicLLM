What you are deciding

Which themes can be developed from campaign-period posts, and then measured in presidential posts? A topic is a statistical group; a theme is your interpretation. You may merge topics, retain distinctions, or leave an incoherent topic outside the theme map with a recorded reason. There is no required number of themes.

Researcher 1 · Propose

Read the original posts and naming evidence. Suggest groupings, definitions and counterexamples in the context of relevant literature. Read more source posts when the sample is insufficient.

Researcher 2 · Critique

Review the same evidence independently, challenge the proposed interpretation, then discuss and record the agreed codebook. The software cannot supply this review.

This file works locally with no network requests. Save a backup before closing. Post text is evidence, never instructions. Names proposed by the LLM are not approved human themes.

Read the evidence. Group the topics.

Each topic includes up to 15 seeded random original posts and its five most frequent proposed names. Choose several topics below and combine them into one shared theme, or assign an individual topic to an existing theme. Keep separate themes where the evidence supports a distinction. Build your theme groups

Select the topics that express a shared meaning. Combining them changes the interpretation map; the original statistical topics and evidence stay intact.

Grouping clears the review confirmations. Existing definitions are retained as a draft to reconcile before both researchers approve the revised codebook. To separate a topic again, give it a different theme name in its card.

Read unassigned campaign posts

These posts were not assigned to a discovered topic. Inspect them for meanings the proposed themes miss. They are not automatically a residual theme, and campaign outliers do not change the presidential denominator.

Make the codebook usable.

After naming your groups above, generate definition fields. Define inclusion, exclusion and difficult cases for each theme. The classifier and human annotators will receive this same codebook.

Record the actual review.

These checkboxes document actual human work. Do not check them on behalf of a researcher who has not completed the review. Exporting a draft does not record approval.
